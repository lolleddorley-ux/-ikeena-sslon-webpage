About Us

Beauty, Fashion, and Elegance in One Place

Who We Are

The IkEe & IKEeNa Empire is a professional Boutique and Beauty Salon committed to bringing confidence, beauty, elegance, and quality services to every customer.

We specialize in Hair installation, Professional Hair Braiding, Advanced Nail Artistry, Professional Makeup, Pedicure & Manicure, and Handmade Beaded Fashion Accessories including Bags, Slippers, Necklaces, Bracelets,and Earrings.

Our Mission

To provide excellent beauty and fashion services using creativity, professionalism, quality products, and outstanding customer care that leaves every client confident and satisfied.

Our Vision

To become The world's leading beauty and fashion brand for excellence, innovation, creativity,and customer satisfaction.
